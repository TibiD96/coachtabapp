CoachTab

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

In short
  • We collect only what the app needs to work: your name and email from Google sign-in, and the workouts you plan or log.
  • Your data is stored in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • Your trainer sees the workouts you log; nobody else does.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell your data.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time.

1. Who is responsible for your data

CoachTab is developed and operated by Tiberiu Daniel Fazakas, an individual developer based in Cluj-Napoca, Romania ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy.

Contact for any privacy question or request: coachtabsupport@gmail.com

2. What data we collect

From Google sign-in

  • Your name and email address
  • A technical account identifier

We never see or store your Google password.

Created when you use the app

  • Your personal referral code, used to invite clients
  • Your trainer–client connections, including when they started and ended
  • Workouts a trainer plans for you or you plan for your clients: dates, titles, notes, exercises, sets, target repetitions and weights
  • Results a client logs: repetitions and weights actually performed, sets marked as failed, exercises and workouts marked as finished, with their timestamps

When you send us feedback

If you use "Send feedback" in Settings, your email app sends us a normal email. It includes your email address, your message, and the app version and phone model/Android version that the app fills in for you. You can remove that information before sending.

When the app crashes

If the app crashes or hits an unexpected error, it sends an automatic report so we can fix the problem. The report contains the technical error and where in our code it happened, the app version, your phone model, Android version, language and time zone, and the names of the screens you visited just before.

The report is deliberately stripped of anything that identifies you: no name, email, user or account identifier, no referral code, no client names and no workout data. Your IP address is not stored.

What we don't collect

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers. The app contains no advertising or analytics software, and we don't track how you use the app.

4. Who can see your data

Inside the app

  • Your trainer, while you are connected, sees your name, the workouts they planned for you and everything you log in them. After you leave your trainer or they remove you, they keep access to the workout history you created together.
  • Your clients, if you are a trainer, see your name and the workouts you plan for them.
  • Anyone who has your referral code can connect to you as your client. Share it only with people you want to train.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers that process data on our behalf, under data processing agreements:

  • Supabase (Supabase Inc.): database and login service. Your data is stored on servers in Frankfurt, Germany (EU). Supabase is a US company and its staff may access data for support and operations; such access is covered by Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission.
  • Google (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC): Google sign-in. Google processes your sign-in under its own Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.): stores and delivers the exercise images and animations. Like any website, it receives your device's IP address when an image loads. No account data is sent to it.
  • Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.): receives the crash reports described above. Our account stores them in the European Union, and IP address storage is switched off.

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with anyone else, and we do not use it for advertising. We would disclose data to authorities only where the law requires it.

5. How long we keep your data

  • We keep your account and workout data for as long as you have an account.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your profile, your trainer–client connections and the workouts connected to your account within 30 days. Encrypted backups are overwritten automatically and are not restored except to recover from a technical failure.
  • Crash reports are deleted automatically by Sentry after its retention period, at most 90 days.
  • Feedback emails are kept for as long as needed to handle your request, and at most 2 years.

6. Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • delete your data ("right to be forgotten");
  • receive your data in a machine-readable format (portability);
  • object to or ask us to restrict processing based on legitimate interest.

To use any of these rights, email coachtabsupport@gmail.com. We will answer within one month. To delete your account, see Delete your CoachTab account.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In Romania this is the National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP), www.dataprotection.ro. You can also contact the authority in the EU country where you live.

7. Security

All communication between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS). Database access rules ensure that each user can only read and change the data they are allowed to. Your sign-in session is stored in your phone's encrypted secure storage.

8. Age requirement

CoachTab is intended for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you believe a child under 16 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If the changes are significant, for example new types of data or new providers, we will also inform you in the app before they take effect.

10. Exercise media

Exercise images and animations are licensed from ExerciseDB and remain their property. They are shown only inside the app, next to the exercise they belong to.
