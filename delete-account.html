Delete your CoachTab account

Last updated: 30 October 2026

You can delete your CoachTab account and the data connected to it at any time, in the app or by email.

Option 1: In the app

  1. Open CoachTab and sign in.
  2. Tap the Settings (gear) button on the home screen.
  3. Scroll to the bottom, tap Delete account, and confirm by typing DELETE.

Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Option 2: By email

If you no longer have the app, send an email to coachtabsupport@gmail.com with the subject CoachTab account deletion, from the email address you use to sign in to CoachTab.

We will confirm and delete your account within 30 days. If the request comes from a different address, we may ask you to confirm that the account is yours.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email address and referral code
  • Your sign-in account
  • All your trainer–client connections
  • Workouts in which you were the client, including everything you logged
  • Workouts you created as a trainer. Your former clients will no longer see them.

What may be kept

  • Encrypted backups are overwritten automatically within 30 days. They are not restored except to recover from a technical failure.
  • Emails you sent us (feedback or this request) are kept for up to 2 years, as described in the Privacy Policy.

Deleting the app from your phone does not delete your account. Use one of the options above.
